AI Readiness Self-Assessment

In thirty years of consulting I have watched organizations make the same mistake with every major technology shift. They start with the tool and work backwards to the problem. AI is no different, except the stakes are higher and the decisions are being made faster than anyone expected.

This assessment asks the four questions most leaders skip. They are not complicated. But they are harder to answer honestly than most people expect — especially about their own organization.

Three things worth knowing before you begin:

This is not a quiz. There are no correct answers.
Your result is a diagnostic, not a grade. It is only useful if your answers reflect how your organization actually operates, not how you would like it to.
Set aside ten minutes. Answer as the leader of your organization, not as someone who has been thinking about AI.
