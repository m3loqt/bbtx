EXECUTIVE SUMMARY



The decision: proceed, but do not pursue a generic
“consulting firm” ranking strategy
  Bottom line

The opportunity exists because the local search market is fragmented. No single boutique firm appears to own
every consulting category. Search results divide among organizational development firms, coaches, strategy
specialists, directories, university resources, and niche practices. That fragmentation lowers the barrier to entry, but
it also means BBTx must establish a clear category before it can become the broad “top consulting firm.”

consistently. The current site contains strong service pages, credible testimonials, a senior-led delivery model, and

description, and Chamber profile still pull the entity toward “AI consulting.” This conflicts with the intended position:
Grant is the product; AI is one tool in his kit. [B01-B09]

correct the category, expand Grant’s authority page, publish outcome-based case studies, align all local profiles,

credibly produce.

Research design
A mechanism-level study of what creates local discoverability, authority, and commercial action

The research question

  Primary question
  What makes a founder-led consulting practice visible, credible, and commercially persuasive in a local market,
  and which of those mechanisms can BBTx apply authentically around Grant Tate’s expertise?


The study does not ask which consulting website looks best. A polished site can be commercially weak, and a plain
site can rank because of age, reviews, referrals, or local reputation. The analysis therefore separates design
preference from observable mechanisms: category clarity, query relevance, founder proof, local prominence, client
evidence, content quality, conversion design, and technical/entity readiness.

Operational definition of “top ranked”
There is no single permanent number-one position for a local consulting firm. Local results vary by the searcher’s
location, the exact query, device, history, and available competitors. Google describes local results as mainly driven
by relevance, distance, and prominence. Distance is only partly controllable, so the strategy concentrates on
relevance and prominence. [G01]
