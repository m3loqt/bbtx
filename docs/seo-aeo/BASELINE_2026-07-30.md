Every non-branded/service query has **zero clicks**: "ai leadership development consulting" (position 35.09), "organizational ai assessment" (position 35), "business consulting firm" (position 9), etc.

## What this confirms

Non-branded search visibility is effectively zero. This is first-party quantification of the report's central premise — and it's a gap technical work (TECH-001/TECH-002) supports but doesn't close. Closing it depends on the human-gated content/positioning work (POS-002, AUT-001/002, CAS-001/002).
